# Gradient Atlas design context

## Design Context

### Users

The primary learner is Mushfique, an undergraduate who learns by testing a
concrete analogy until its edge cases break. The public audience is a serious
undergraduate or self-learner who wants enough mathematical and systems depth
to read papers, question benchmark claims, and start research instead of merely
recognizing AI vocabulary.

The main jobs are to repair an incomplete mental model, move between intuition
and formal notation, manipulate a visual model, solve a checkable problem, and
trace a frontier claim back to primary evidence.

### Brand personality

Rigorous, exploratory, visually lucid. The writing should feel like a demanding
teacher who respects a good question but never rewards it with false certainty.

### Aesthetic direction

An editorial scientific field guide. Use Algebrica's restrained reading rhythm
as a reference: light paper-like surfaces, black typography, clear notation,
thin rules, and diagrams that earn their space. Add a small amount of amber and
blue only when color encodes a distinction such as observed versus predicted,
train-time versus inference-time, or verified versus speculative.

Avoid a dashboard shell, purple gradients, glass effects, stock card grids,
decorative neural-network imagery, glow, generic technical monospace styling,
and animation that does not explain a state change.

### Design principles

1. Start each concept with a manipulable question, not a marketing hero.
2. Put intuition, failure boundary, formal object, and counterexample in view.
3. Make evidence status visible but quiet; never turn citations into decoration.
4. Keep long reading lines between 65 and 75 characters and give notation more
   space than prose.
5. Adapt diagrams for touch and narrow screens without hiding their controls.
6. Motion must reveal computation or transition and respect reduced-motion
   preferences.

### Typography procedure

Brand words: rigorous, exploratory, lucid.

Rejected reflex fonts: Geist, Inter, IBM Plex, Newsreader, Fraunces, and the
other default families listed by the Impeccable skill. The final pair must come
from a browsed catalog, remain legible for long study sessions, support math
symbols, and have a local or redistribution-safe web delivery path.
